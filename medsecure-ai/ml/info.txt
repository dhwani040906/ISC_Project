### Final Test-Set Evaluation of the Pneumonia CNN

The proposed convolutional neural network was evaluated on the official PneumoniaMNIST test set, consisting of 624 images: 234 normal cases and 390 pneumonia cases. The best checkpoint was selected using validation loss, and a classification threshold of 0.40 was selected using the validation set.

The model achieved an accuracy of 83.01%, precision of 79.10%, sensitivity of 98.97%, specificity of 56.41%, and an F1-score of 0.8793. The ROC-AUC was 0.9543, while Average Precision was 0.9637.

The confusion matrix contained 386 true positives, 132 true negatives, 102 false positives, and 4 false negatives. These results indicate that the model detected most pneumonia cases at the chosen threshold but incorrectly classified a substantial number of normal cases as pneumonia.

Compared with the previously evaluated threshold of 0.50, the threshold of 0.40 increased sensitivity while reducing specificity. This illustrates the trade-off between detecting positive cases and limiting false alarms. Further analysis should investigate threshold selection on validation data and assess generalization before considering any clinical application.

The system is an educational research prototype and is not intended for clinical diagnosis.
